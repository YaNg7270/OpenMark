# Deeply Nested Unordered List

- Level one groups the high-level phases of the storage migration.
  - Level two separates the inventory work from the execution work.
    - Level three breaks inventory into discovery and classification.
      - Level four records the individual export commands and their required ordering.
      - Level four also records the verification step that must follow each export.
    - Level three breaks execution into dry runs and the live cutover.
  - Level two collects the rollback notes in case a phase must be undone.
- Level one also contains a second branch for post-migration monitoring.
