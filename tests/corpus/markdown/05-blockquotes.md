# Blockquotes

A blockquote is introduced by a greater-than sign followed by an optional space.

> A simple quote that contains exactly one paragraph of quoted material.

Quotes may be nested by repeating the marker at each level.

> The outer level introduces a topic that the inner levels will refine.
>
> > The second level narrows the claim and adds a supporting detail.
> >
> > > The third level states the precise technical caveat that the first two levels omitted.

A quote may hold any block construct, including lists.

> Checklist for reviewing a blockquote:
>
> - the marker is followed by a space
> - nested levels repeat the marker
> - blank quoted lines still carry the marker
> - lazy continuation lines are accepted

A quote may also hold a fenced code block.

> The following snippet shows the marker used in source form.
>
> ```text
> > first level
> >> second level
> ```
